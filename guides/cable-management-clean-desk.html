Cable Management, Made Simple: The 5-Step Routine for a Clean Desk

By The Cozy Desk Space · Desk setup guides

Cables are the difference between a desk that looks 'fine' and a desk that looks finished. This is the exact routine we use — no drilling required, one afternoon, and supplies that typically cost less than a single new gadget.

The 5-step clean cable routine

1

Unplug everything

Yes, everything. You cannot organise a rat's nest one cable at a time — start from zero.

2

Mount the power strip

Get it off the floor. Stick or screw it under the desk, near the back edge.

3

Route through a tray

Every cable runs inside an under-desk cable tray first, then up to its device.

4

Bundle & label

Velcro ties every 20–30 cm. Label both ends — future you says thanks.

5

One visible cable max

The goal: from your chair, you should see at most one cable (your charging lead).

The only supplies you actually need

Under-desk cable management tray

typically $15–$30

Clamp-on trays need no drilling — perfect for rented rooms and dorms. Measure your desk thickness before buying.

Velcro cable ties (reusable)

typically $6–$12

Never use zip ties on cables you move often. Velcro is re-openable, gentle and cheap.

Adhesive cable clips

typically $7–$12

For the one charging cable you keep visible: clips along the desk edge keep it from sliding to the floor.

Cable sleeve or spiral wrap

typically $8–$15

Merges the monitor/power/HDMI trio into one neat 'trunk' running down to the tray.

Mistakes that make cables look worse

  • Tight zip ties — they kink cables and are a pain to undo.
  • A tray that is too small — bricks and adapters need room; size up.
  • Forgetting slack — sit/stand desks need a service loop so cables are not stretched at full height.
  • Hiding a hot power brick in a closed box — bricks need airflow; open trays beat closed boxes.
Cozy tip: Photograph your cables before you start. The before/after is the most satisfying part — and on a bad work day, the 'after' photo is proof your space is more in control than your inbox.
